Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I request a leave of absence?
A: Submit the leave application through the student portal and get approval from the academic advisor.

Q: Where can I check my exam timetable?
A: The timetable is published on the student portal and notices board.

Q: Can I submit a project late?
A: Late submission is allowed only with tutor approval and may incur a grade penalty.

Q: What should I do if I miss a class?
A: Review the lecture notes, contact the lecturer, and catch up on any missed work.
